Proof of Field

Farmer-owned deforestation proofs,
sold agent-to-agent for stablecoins.

Murilo Leite · Nixar Solutions · farmer in Abunã, Rondônia
Built on HSK Chain

1

To sell soy to a trading company, Zé has to prove his 5,500 ha did not deforest since 2020.

  • Who asks: importers (EUDR puts the legal burden on them), banks for credit, and now Chinese buyers.
  • How it works today: hire a certifier, hand over the entire farm map and documents, wait weeks, pay again for every buyer.
  • What he loses: time, money, and control of the most sensitive data he owns.

Slow. Expensive. And the farmer never owns the proof.

2
#1

China is the largest buyer of Brazilian soy and beef in the world.

Every shipment needs a deforestation-free proof for every farm it came from. Traders and banks already pay per farm for that check.

The market is not "should farms be checked". It is who does the check, who gets paid, and who keeps the data.

3
Live demo

Draw the field. Check it against INPE. Sign. Anchor. Sell.

  • Official INPE / PRODES deforestation polygons, intersected in ~100 ms.
  • Verdict signed by the farmer (EIP-712), hash anchored on HSK Chain.
  • The polygon never leaves the farmer's machine.

How it works

Pure geometry against INPE's public polygons. No ML, no cloud.

farmer · privateEIP-712 attestation

Area, ha deforested after 2020, verdict, source, timestamp. Signed.

HSK ChainAnchor

attest(hash, fieldId) in the registry. Only commitments on-chain.

buyer agentHTTP 402 → pay

x402 flow. Pays USDT through an AgentWallet with spending policy.

buyer agentVerify

Signature → same farmer anchored it → not revoked. No trust in us.

No human in the loop. The farmer is paid the second his data is used, and decides who gets it.

5

Why Ethereum, why HSK

  • Machines don't have bank accounts. A stablecoin is the only way one program pays another, automatically.
  • Public registry → any buyer, bank or auditor verifies without trusting the farmer, a certifier, or us.
  • AgentWallet → a company funds an agent once, sets daily and per-payment limits and allowed payees, and never hands over its keys.
  • HSK Chain: EVM, cheap, and where the Asian buyer side lives.
6

Open source, reusable, and where this goes

Reusable today

  • FieldAttestationRegistry — anchor any signed claim about private data.
  • AgentWallet — policy-enforced spending for autonomous agents.
  • x402 seller server — turn any private dataset into a paid, verifiable proof.

Next

  • EIP-3009 gasless settlement, x402 facilitator.
  • ZK proof that field ∩ PRODES = ∅ — even the hectares stay private.
  • Sentinel-2 change detection between PRODES releases, on the farmer's machine.
  • Second asset: cattle passports for beef traceability.
